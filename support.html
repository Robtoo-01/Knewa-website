Get help

We are here when you need us

Whatever is happening, reach out. Members can ask the committee for support at any time, and every request is treated in confidence.

In danger or a medical emergency? Call 000 now. KENEWA: 0475 140 471. Need to talk to someone right away? Call Lifeline on 13 11 14, any time, day or night.

Talk to the committee

For urgent matters such as a death in the family or a hospital admission, call us straight away.

KENEWA help line: call, text or WhatsApp 0475 140 471

Or email info@kenewa.org. The help line goes to a committee member, who will connect you with the right person.

Not urgent? Members can send a written request from their members area. A committee member will reply, usually within two days.

  • NSW Mental Health Line 1800 011 511Mental health advice and referral, 24 hours

Members: request support online

Log in to your members area to send a confidential request and follow its progress. Only the committee members handling it will see it.
