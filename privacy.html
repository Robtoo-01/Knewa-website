Privacy policy

How we look after your information

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Kenyans in New England Welfare Association Inc (KENEWA, ABN 17 238 522 745) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it. We aim to follow the Australian Privacy Principles as good practice.

What we collect

When you contact us, apply for membership or ask for welfare support, we may collect your name, contact details, town, the names of family members you register, and information you choose to share about your circumstances.

Why we collect it

  • To manage memberships and keep a register of members
  • To provide welfare support and assess requests fairly
  • To tell members about meetings, events and news
  • To reply to enquiries
  • To meet our legal and governance obligations

Sensitive information

When you ask for welfare support you may tell us about health, illness, a death in the family or money troubles, and you may attach documents such as a hospital letter or death certificate. This is sensitive information. We only collect it with your consent, which you give by sending the request, and we use it only to consider and provide support. Only the committee members handling your request can see it.

Who can see it

Only committee members who need it for the purposes above. Welfare matters are kept confidential. We do not sell or rent your information. We will not share it outside KENEWA unless you agree or the law requires it.

How we store it

Member accounts, registration details, family details, payment records and support requests are stored in a secure online database hosted in Sydney, Australia. Members can see only their own records; only authorised committee members can see members' records. Documents you attach to a support request are kept in a private store that only you and the committee can open. Paper records are kept securely.

The Treasurer or the Secretary may download a copy of the members register as a spreadsheet. Each download needs approval from another committee member, every download is recorded, and copies are stored securely and deleted when no longer needed.

Services that handle information for us

Some of the services we use are run by companies that may store information outside Australia:

  • Messages sent through our contact form are received by our website host (Netlify).
  • Emails we send to members, such as registration confirmations, go through an email service (Resend).
  • If you pay by card, your payment is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Members' documents, such as the constitution and minutes, may be shared through Google Drive.

We choose services with good security and only give them what they need to do their job.

How long we keep it

We keep your membership details while you are a member. After your membership ends we keep only what we need for our financial records and legal obligations, then delete it. Support requests and attached documents are deleted once they are no longer needed for the request or for our records.

Photos

We may take photos at events to share on this website and our social media. Let an organiser know if you or your children do not want to be photographed, and contact us if you would like a photo removed.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting the Secretary. You can unsubscribe from member messages at any time.

Website visits

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add visitor statistics, we will use a privacy-friendly service and update this policy.

Questions and complaints

Questions or concerns about privacy: email info@kenewa.org or call 0475 140 471 and ask for the Secretary. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
