Privacy policy

How we look after your information

Last updated: October 2026

Kenyans in New England Welfare Association Inc (KNEWA, ABN 17 238 522 745) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it. We aim to follow the Australian Privacy Principles as good practice.

What we collect

When you contact us, apply for membership or ask for welfare support, we may collect your name, contact details, town, the names of family members you register, and information you choose to share about your circumstances.

Why we collect it

  • To manage memberships and keep a register of members
  • To provide welfare support and assess requests fairly
  • To tell members about meetings, events and news
  • To reply to enquiries
  • To meet our legal and governance obligations

Who can see it

Only committee members who need it for the purposes above. Welfare matters are kept confidential. We do not sell or rent your information. We will not share it outside KNEWA unless you agree or the law requires it.

How we store it

Website form submissions are received through our web host's secure form service and stored in accounts protected by passwords. Paper records are kept securely. We keep information only as long as we need it.

Photos

We may take photos at events to share on this website and our social media. Let an organiser know if you or your children do not want to be photographed, and contact us if you would like a photo removed.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting the Secretary. You can unsubscribe from member messages at any time.

Website visits

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add visitor statistics, we will use a privacy-friendly service and update this policy.

Contact

Questions or concerns about privacy: secretary@kenewa.org.
