Join KNEWA

Join the KNEWA family

Join the Kenyan community of the New England region: welfare support when you need it, a vote in how KNEWA is run, and people who have your back.

Membership

Who can join

Membership is open to Kenyans living in or connected with the New England region who support the association's objectives. Financial members must be 18 or over; people under 18 can join with a parent or guardian's written consent.

Types of membership

  • Financial member: pays the joining fees, can vote at general meetings, stand for the committee and receive welfare support.
  • Community member: free. Receives news and can attend events, but cannot vote or hold office.
  • Life and honorary members: recognised by the association for outstanding service.

What you get

  • Welfare fund support in times of need
  • A vote at the Annual General Meeting
  • First news of events and opportunities
  • A welcoming network across the region
