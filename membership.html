Become a welfare member

Who can join

Membership is open to Kenyans living in or connected with the New England region who support the association's objectives. Anyone aged 18 or over can apply for welfare (financial) membership. People under 18 can apply with a parent or guardian's written consent, on terms the committee sets.

Types of membership

  • Welfare (financial) member: pays the joining fees, can vote at general meetings, stand for the committee and receive welfare support.
  • Community member: free. Receives news and can attend events, but cannot vote or hold office.
  • Life members: recognised by a general meeting for outstanding service. They have the same rights as welfare members and pay no fees.
  • Honorary members: invited by the committee in recognition of service. They can attend and speak at general meetings, but cannot vote or hold office.

The full rules are in our constitution (PDF).

Renewing your membership

Welfare membership lasts 12 months from the day the Treasurer confirms your payment. We'll send you a reminder before it's due. The whole $170 renewal fee goes into the welfare fund.

If your renewal is more than 3 months late, you can't vote at general meetings, and welfare support and member services pause until you pay. If money is tight, talk to the committee: they can make exceptions in cases of hardship.

Welfare support starts once your membership has been current for 3 months, except for a bereavement or life-threatening emergency. See Welfare support.

How to become a member

  1. Check you're eligible

    Read who can join on this page, and decide whether you want financial or community membership.

  2. Create your account and choose your membership

    Sign up with your name, email, mobile and a password, and confirm your email address. The first thing you'll be asked is whether you want welfare or community membership.

  3. Complete your registration

    In the members area, fill in your details and next of kin.

  4. Register your family

    Financial members add up to 1 spouse, 7 dependants and 7 siblings, then confirm the list. Your first list is approved automatically; later additions, changes or removals are checked by the committee.

  5. Pay and record your fees

    Financial members pay $200 by bank transfer, PayID, card or cash, then record the payment in the members area. Your welfare member services unlock as soon as the Treasurer confirms it.

  6. Committee approval

    The committee approves your membership and gives you a member number. You'll see it on your dashboard.

What members receive

Benefits marked Welfare members are for financial members whose joining fees have been confirmed by the Treasurer. Some programs are still being set up in KENEWA's first year.

Bereavement support Welfare members

Financial help from the welfare fund when a member or registered family member dies, including funeral costs in Australia or repatriation to Kenya.

Help in illness and emergencies Welfare members

Hospital visits, meals, transport and, where approved, financial help after serious illness, injury, fire, flood or sudden job loss.

Direct support line

Request help any time from your members area, or call the committee through the Get help page. Every request is handled confidentially.

A vote and a voice Welfare members

Vote online and at general meetings, stand for the committee, and help decide how KENEWA and its welfare fund are run. Online votes are secret.

Digital membership card Welfare members

Your membership card and member number on your phone, ready to show or print.

Members' documents Welfare members

The constitution, meeting minutes, financial reports and the welfare fund policy, all in one place.

Coming soon Welfare members

A chama savings circle, interest-free emergency loans, education bursaries, a mentoring network, a member business directory and partner discounts.

Celebrating your milestones

The community marks weddings, new babies, graduations and other happy moments with you.

Settling in

Practical help for newcomers: housing, jobs, Medicare, Centrelink, licences and school enrolment, plus introductions to local services.

Events and celebrations

Madaraka, Mashujaa and Jamhuri Day celebrations, family days and sports days, with member prices or free entry where possible.

Jobs and networking

Job leads, CV help and contacts across health, agriculture, education, trades and business in the region.

Youth and students

Orientation for international students, mentoring for young people, and a student representative on the committee.

Women's empowerment

A women’s network and savings circles in the chama tradition.

Wellbeing

Mental health and wellbeing sessions, and referrals to trusted services when life gets heavy.

Members-only updates

Member news, the WhatsApp group, the quarterly newsletter and early notice of events and opportunities.
