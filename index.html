Why join KNEWA

More than membership, it's belonging

Membership means people who know you, a plan for when life gets hard, and plenty to celebrate along the way. Here's what you gain when you join.

Become a member

Welfare in hard times

Members contribute to a welfare fund that supports families through bereavement, including funeral and repatriation costs, serious illness and emergencies.

A welcome for new arrivals

Help settling in: housing, jobs, Medicare and Centrelink, licences, schools, and introductions to local settlement services.

Culture and national days

Madaraka Day, Mashujaa Day and Jamhuri Day celebrations, and Kenyan food, music and language shared with our children and neighbours.

Students and youth

A student representative on the committee, orientation for international students, and mentoring for young Kenyan-Australians.

Women's empowerment

A dedicated committee role and space for women to support each other, including savings and investment circles in the chama tradition.

Sport, wellbeing and networking

Sports days, mental health and wellbeing sessions, and a network for careers, education and business across the region.
