Our vision

A united, inclusive and thriving Kenyan community that supports one another and contributes positively to Australian society.

Our mission is to promote the welfare, unity and empowerment of Kenyans in the New England region through mutual support, community engagement, cultural preservation and collaboration.

  • Umoja Unity One community across every town and background.
  • Upendo Care We look out for one another, especially when it's hard.
  • Heshima Respect For every member, elder, culture and neighbour.
  • Uaminifu Integrity Open, accountable handling of members' contributions.

Why families join KNEWA

Membership means people who know you, a plan for when life gets hard, and plenty to celebrate along the way.

Become a member

Welfare in hard times

Members contribute to a welfare fund that supports families through bereavement, including funeral and repatriation costs, serious illness and emergencies.

A welcome for new arrivals

Help settling in: housing, jobs, Medicare and Centrelink, licences, schools, and introductions to local settlement services.

Culture and national days

Madaraka Day, Mashujaa Day and Jamhuri Day celebrations, and Kenyan food, music and language shared with our children and neighbours.

Students and youth

A student representative on the committee, orientation for international students, and mentoring for young Kenyan-Australians.

Women's empowerment

A dedicated committee role and space for women to support each other, including savings and investment circles in the chama tradition.

Sport, wellbeing and networking

Sports days, mental health and wellbeing sessions, and a network for careers, education and business across the region.

Ready to join the KNEWA family?

Membership is open to Kenyans living in or connected with the New England region who support the association's objectives. Joining takes a few minutes.
