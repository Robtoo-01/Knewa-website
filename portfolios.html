Portfolios

How KENEWA works for you

Like a village council, our work is shared: each portfolio is led by a volunteer committee member, and every member is welcome to help.

Welfare

The committee, with the welfare fund managed by Treasurer Hillary Bett

  • Support for members in bereavement, including funeral and repatriation costs
  • Visits, meals and transport during illness or hospital stays
  • Help after sudden hardship, and links to local services
Ask for support

Women's empowerment

Janet Bett, Women's Empowerment

  • A welcoming network for women across the region
  • Savings and investment circles in the chama tradition
  • Mentoring, wellbeing and leadership opportunities
Join the women's network

Students and youth

Ruth Tanui, Student Representative

  • Orientation and a friendly face for international students
  • Mentoring and role models for young Kenyan-Australians
  • A voice for young people on the committee
Get involved

Sport and wellbeing

Sheldon Kipkemboi, Sport

  • Sports days and friendly matches for all ages and abilities
  • Walking, running and fitness groups
  • Mental health and wellbeing sessions

Culture and events

Geoffrey Seurei, Organising Secretary

Kenyans in New England Welfare Association Inc

Umoja ni nguvu: unity is strength

A not-for-profit, non-political community association promoting the welfare, unity and empowerment of Kenyans in the New England region of NSW.
