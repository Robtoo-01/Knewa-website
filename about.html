About KNEWA

A Kenyan family in New England

Kenyans in New England Welfare Association Inc is a not-for-profit, non-political community association for Kenyans living in or connected with the New England region of NSW.

How it began

Our story

KNEWA began at a founding meeting at 12 Porter Street, East Tamworth, where twelve members of the local Kenyan community came together with one shared commitment: to establish a welfare association for Kenyans living in the New England region. They unanimously resolved to form the Kenyans in New England Welfare Association and elected an interim committee.

In July 2026 the association received its ABN, and on 29 July 2026 it was registered by NSW Fair Trading as an incorporated association under the Associations Incorporation Act 2009. The committee has since confirmed its office bearers and begun building membership through WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Our purpose

  • Promote unity among Kenyans living in the New England region
  • Provide welfare support to members and the wider community
  • Preserve Kenyan culture while supporting integration with Australian life
  • Assist new arrivals to settle
  • Promote networking, education, community development, social support and mental health

Our founding promise

Mission and vision

A united, inclusive and thriving Kenyan community that supports one another and contributes positively to Australian society.

Our mission is to promote the welfare, unity and empowerment of Kenyans in the New England region through mutual support, community engagement, cultural preservation and collaboration.

Our values guide everything we do:

Umoja

Unity

One community across every town and background.

Upendo

Care

We look out for one another, especially when it's hard.

Heshima

Respect

For every member, elder, culture and neighbour.

Uaminifu

Integrity

Open, accountable handling of members' contributions.
