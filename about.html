About us

About our association

Kenyans in New England Welfare Association Inc is a not-for-profit, non-political community association for Kenyans living in or connected with the New England region of NSW.

Who we are

Our story

KENEWA began at a founding meeting at 12 Porter Street, East Tamworth, where twelve members of the local Kenyan community came together with one shared commitment: to establish a welfare association for Kenyans living in the New England region. They unanimously resolved to form the Kenyans in New England Welfare Association and elected an interim committee.

The association received its ABN on 19 July 2026, and on 29 July 2026 NSW Fair Trading registered it as an incorporated association under the Associations Incorporation Act 2009 (INC2601004). At its meeting on 23 August 2026 the committee confirmed its office bearers, agreed the joining fees, approved our social media channels, and started work on grant applications and this website.

Today KENEWA works through seven portfolios, from welfare and women's empowerment to students, sport and culture, each led by a volunteer committee member.

Rules
Constitution and Welfare Fund Policy (PDF)

Our main objective

To stand as a united group for the welfare, representation and empowerment of Kenyans living in or connected with the New England region of New South Wales.

Our mission

To promote the welfare, unity and empowerment of Kenyans in the New England region through mutual support, community engagement, cultural preservation and collaboration.

Our vision

A united, inclusive and thriving Kenyan community that supports one another and contributes positively to Australian society.

Our purpose

  • Promote unity among Kenyans living in the New England region
  • Provide welfare support to members and the wider community
  • Preserve Kenyan culture while supporting integration with Australian life
  • Assist new arrivals to settle
  • Promote networking, education, community development, social support and mental health
  • UmojaUnityOne community across every town and background.
  • UpendoCareWe look out for one another, especially when it's hard.
  • HeshimaRespectFor every member, elder, culture and neighbour.
  • UaminifuIntegrityOpen, accountable handling of members' contributions.
