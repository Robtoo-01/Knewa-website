Support requests

Welfare fund requests: decide within 48 hours for a bereavement or emergency and 7 days otherwise. Two office-bearers can approve up to $500 straight away. Check the fund balance with the Treasurer first: a payment that would take the fund below its $3,000 reserve should go to the full committee (Welfare Fund Policy Amendment No. 1, once adopted; record the decision on form W2). For a death, start the Bereavement and Repatriation Response Plan (form W5).
