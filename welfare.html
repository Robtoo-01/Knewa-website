Welfare support

When life is hard, we stand together

Welfare support is at the heart of KENEWA. Here is what it covers and how to ask for help.

What support covers

When new members join, $170 of their $200 joining fee goes into the KENEWA welfare fund, and so does the whole $170 annual renewal fee. This is the modern form of the Kenyan tradition of harambee: pulling together so that no family carries a crisis alone. Support is available to welfare (financial) members and their registered family, and the committee decides each case fairly under the Welfare Fund Policy (PDF).

Bereavement

Financial and practical help when a member or registered family member passes away, including funeral costs in Australia or repatriation to Kenya, meals, transport and liaising with family at home.

Serious illness or injury

Visits, meals, transport and, where approved, financial assistance during hospitalisation or long recovery.

Sudden hardship

Help after emergencies such as fire, flood, family violence or job loss, for example paying an urgent bill on your behalf, plus links to local services.

New arrivals

Practical help for newly arrived Kenyan families: a welcome pack, help setting up essential services, short-term food or household items, and introductions to settlement services.

Outside the welfare fund, the community also celebrates happy milestones with you: weddings, new babies and graduations.

How to ask for help

  1. Contact a committee member

    Call a committee member or send a request through the Get help page. Family or friends can ask on a member's behalf.

  2. We listen and check details

    A committee member confirms membership and what support is needed. We may ask for a document such as a funeral quote or hospital letter, but in an emergency we can help first and collect it later. Everything is kept confidential.

  3. The committee decides

    Within 48 hours for a bereavement or emergency, and within 7 days for other requests. We tell you the decision in writing and, if support is reduced or declined, why.

  4. Support is given

    Where possible we pay the funeral director, landlord or other provider directly. If needed, members may also be asked to give to a community collection.

How much support?

These are the most the welfare fund can give for each event. The committee decides the amount based on need and what the fund can afford, so meeting the rules does not guarantee the full amount. The fund tops up other help, such as insurance, Centrelink or a funeral fund, and we'll help you apply for those too.
