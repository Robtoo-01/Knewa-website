Bereavement · Illness · Hardship

When life is hard, we stand together

Welfare support is at the heart of KNEWA. Here is what it covers and how to ask for help.

Harambee

What support covers

When new members join, $170 of their $200 joining fee goes into the KNEWA welfare fund. This is the modern form of the Kenyan tradition of harambee: pulling together so that no family carries a crisis alone. Support is available to financial members, and the committee decides each case fairly.

Bereavement

Financial and practical help when a member or close family member passes away, including funeral costs in Australia or repatriation to Kenya, meals, transport and liaising with family at home.

Serious illness or injury

Visits, meals, transport and, where approved, financial assistance during hospitalisation or long recovery.

Sudden hardship

Help after emergencies such as fire, flood or job loss, including links to local services.

Celebrations

We also mark happy milestones: weddings, new babies and graduations.

Step by step

How to ask for help

  1. Contact a committee member

    Call or message the Chairperson, Secretary or any committee member, or use the contact form. Family or friends can contact us on a member's behalf.

  2. We listen and check details

    A committee member confirms membership and what support is needed. Everything is kept confidential.

  3. The committee decides

    Urgent cases, such as a bereavement, are handled as quickly as possible.

  4. Members mobilise

    Support is given and, where needed, members are asked to contribute through the agreed welfare process.

Need help right now?

If someone is in immediate danger, call 000. For crisis support any time, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.
