Our journey so far

  1. Founding meeting

    Twelve founding members meet at 12 Porter Street, East Tamworth, and unanimously resolve to establish the Kenyans in New England Welfare Association, electing an interim committee.

  2. 19 July 2026: ABN registered

    The association receives its Australian Business Number.

  3. 29 July 2026: incorporated

    NSW Fair Trading registers Kenyans in New England Welfare Association Inc as an incorporated association (INC2601004).

  4. 23 August 2026: committee meeting

    Office bearers confirmed, joining fees agreed, social media channels approved, and work started on grant applications and this website.
